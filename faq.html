QUESTIONS

Answers from the work we have published.

Prices, place and how to order are below. Anything we do not know is marked TODO and left blank on purpose.

Where is Krint Tufwale based?

Krint Tufwale is based in Lusaka, Zambia. The clothing is designed in Zambia.

Who founded Krint Tufwale?

John Mambwe founded Krint Tufwale in Lusaka, Zambia.

What does Krint Tufwale sell?

Contemporary everyday clothing. The collections are Everyday, Tailored and Essentials. The shop is grouped into shirts, trousers, outerwear, knitwear and accessories.

What currency are prices in?

Prices are in Zambian kwacha (ZMW). The figure on each piece is the catalogue price. When an order is placed, the server recalculates the total from that catalogue.

How do I place an order?

Create an account and add a piece to your bag. Product pages say checkout with mobile money is coming soon and that, for now, orders are placed by email. The contact address published on the site is hello@krinttufwale.com.

Is there a search box for the catalogue?

No. The shop page filters pieces by shirts, trousers, outerwear, knitwear and accessories.

What sizes are available?

Cotton Overshirt, Straight Leg Trouser, Classic Cotton Shirt, Fine Knit Polo, Structured Jacket, Everyday Polo, Relaxed Pleated Trouser are listed in S, M, L, XL. Leather Belt is One Size. A body-measurement chart is not published yet.
